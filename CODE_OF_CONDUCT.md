# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Matchwell's project community a
respectful, safe, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

Participation in this repository also requires respect for Matchwell's
Christian product mission. Disagreement must never become harassment,
dehumanization, discrimination, or mistreatment.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- demonstrating empathy, kindness, and respect;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully;
- focusing discussion on the project's requirements and evidence;
- respecting privacy, confidentiality, and safety boundaries;
- acknowledging mistakes and learning from them; and
- acting in the best interests of the project community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks;
- public or private harassment, threats, or intimidation;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit
  permission;
- discriminatory conduct or advocacy of mistreatment;
- attempting to obtain or disclose member, counselor, screening, payment,
  authentication, or other sensitive data; and
- conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
commits, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone officially
represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through direct
GitHub contact. Do not open a public issue containing personal, sensitive, or
safety-related details.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this
policy. Reports will be reviewed promptly, fairly, and with confidentiality
appropriate to the circumstances. Retaliation against a reporter is
prohibited.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will consider the context, severity, recurrence, safety impact,
and demonstrated willingness to correct behavior. Responses may include a
private correction, a formal warning, temporary restriction, or permanent
removal from project participation.
